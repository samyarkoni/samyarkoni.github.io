Research note · Quantum Route

Who drives an NFL offense: the coach or the quarterback?

A Bayesian variance decomposition over 4,254 team-games from the 2018–2025 regular seasons, built on the Quantum Route modeling stack.

Why “Quantum Route”

Before the snap, a drive could take any route. The model plays out many of them and weighs each by probability. The drive that actually happens is just one of them.

How the model is built

  1. 1st
    Play-by-play dataEight seasons (2018–2025) from nflverse, one file per game and a row per play.
  2. 2nd
    Expected pointsAn EP/EPA model values every play by how it changes the scoring outlook.
  3. 3rd
    Team strength and coaching tendenciesBayesian models fit with a hand-written Metropolis-within-Gibbs sampler, checked with split R-hat and effective sample size.
  4. 4th
    Drive and game simulatorSimulates play calls, yardage and field goals to produce full-game outcomes.
  5. TD
    Walk-forward backtestEach week is predicted only from earlier games, then scored against closing spreads, totals and moneylines.

Headline result

Efficiency follows the quarterback. Fourth-down decisions follow the head coach.

Head coachStarting QBRest of roster and staff
  • One standard deviation of QB quality is worth about 0.076 EPA per play, roughly 4.7 points a game. The same for a head coach is about 0.030, or 1.9 points.
  • Pass rate over expected tracks the QB more than the coach, which suggests coaches shape the play mix around the quarterback they have.

My app · Nyte Nyte

Nyte Nyte listens before you sleep.

“what’s still awake in you?”

I'm building Nyte Nyte for the hour before bed. You talk through whatever is on your mind, and it helps you set it down so tomorrow starts clearer.

  • Catches spirals. Notices rumination and catastrophizing, names the pattern gently, and offers a reframe and grounding steps.
  • Plans tomorrow. Turns what you said into an intention, a timeline and concrete tasks.
  • Does the work overnight. Hands research, drafts and study guides to a Claude agent with web search, ready by morning.
  • Maps your dreams. Builds a knowledge graph of people, places and themes that connects across nights.

Early interest

After I pitched Nyte Nyte, the professor of my Honors seminar, From Distractions to Mind Wandering, wanted to bring it into her class.
