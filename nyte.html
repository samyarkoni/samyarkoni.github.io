How Nyte Nyte works

“what’s still awake in you?”

Nyte Nyte is an iOS app I'm building for the hour before bed. You say whatever is on your mind, it listens, and by morning the night has turned into something you can use. Here is each part of it, using screens from the app.

01The Dump

Say it before you sleep on it.

Everything starts on the Dump screen. There are two ways in: Before sleep, for whatever is still running through your head at night, and A dream, for writing down what you dreamed.

You can type, or tap the mic and just talk. Nothing has to be organized. Sorting it out is Nyte's job.

02Catching spirals

It notices when a thought starts to loop.

Nighttime thinking can spiral: the same worry on repeat (rumination), or jumping straight to the worst case (catastrophizing). Nyte watches for both while you talk.

NoticeIt picks up on rumination and catastrophizing.
Name itIt points out the pattern gently, without judgment.
SteadyIt offers a reframe and a few grounding steps.

03The Dream map

Your nights, drawn as a constellation.

Every dream is broken into its pieces: the people, places, things, actions, feelings and themes in it. Each piece becomes a star, and pieces that show up together are linked. The map builds across nights, so it grows into a picture of what your mind keeps returning to.

  • Color is the kind of symbol.
    personplacethingactionfeelingtheme
  • Brighter means it comes back more often. A symbol that keeps appearing night after night glows stronger.
  • Orange rings are worries from what you said before sleep.
  • A dashed line is a possible echo: a worry that may be showing up inside a dream.
  • A solid line is a recurring echo.

04Patterns

What keeps you up, and what has eased.

The Patterns screen steps back from single nights. It has two views, one for your thoughts and one for your dreams. In both, a fuller color means it comes back more often, and you can tap a circle for details.

  • Leads to: one thought tends to bring on another.
  • Competes: two things pulling on the same time or attention.
  • Said together: they came up in the same breath.
  • Eased: every task that came from it is done.

05Overnight

While you sleep, the night turns into a plan.

Once you've said your piece, Nyte takes what you talked about and gets it ready for the morning.

Plans tomorrow

It turns what you said into an intention for the day, a timeline, and concrete tasks.

Does the work

Tasks like research, first drafts, outlines and study guides go to a Claude agent with web search, so they're done by morning.

Keeps your habits

It tracks habit streaks and works your habits into each night's plan.

06The Day side

Morning picks up where the night left off.

Switch from Nyte to Day and the plan is waiting: the intention, the timeline, the tasks, and whatever the agent researched or drafted overnight. As you check tasks off, the worries they came from ease, and Nyte notices.
